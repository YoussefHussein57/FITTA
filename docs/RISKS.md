# FITTA — Risk Register

**Status:** Phase 00, awaiting approval
**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

Scales: Likelihood / Impact = Low · Medium · High. **Severity** is the combination, judged — not
multiplied, because a low-likelihood legal risk can still be existential.

## Risks accepted without mitigation (stated honestly)

| Risk                                        | Why accepted                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No high availability in V1                  | 99.5% single-region is adequate for the audience and stage (NFR-05). HA is a real cost with no V1 benefit                                                      |
| No MFA in V1                                | Argon2id + rate limiting + revocable sessions is a reasonable baseline. The schema leaves room so MFA is additive, not a migration                             |
| No background job infrastructure            | Export and provider sync are synchronous in V1. Revisit when a measured request exceeds its budget                                                             |
| Self-managed auth rather than a managed IdP | Deliberate: it is the most instructive part of the system, and it keeps health-adjacent identity data out of a third party (ADR-005)                           |
| Nutrition values will sometimes be wrong    | Unavoidable in any food database. Mitigated by _honesty_ about provenance rather than a false claim of accuracy — which is the product's actual differentiator |
